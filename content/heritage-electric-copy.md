## What we do

We handle the electrical on luxury new construction and remodels. Most of our
work comes to us through builders and architects we have known for years. A good
part of it is lighting, which means the design, the control system, and the
software that keeps it running afterward.

- Lighting design
- Vantage control systems, including programming and software updates
- Wireless lighting control
- Landscape lighting and design
- Generator installation
- Car charging stations
- Power connections for solar systems
- Plan review and project planning

---

## Lighting control

If you want the lighting in a house to run from a phone, a tablet or a wall
keypad, that is the Vantage side of what we do. We design the system, install
it, program it, and come back to update the software when it needs it.

## Why builders keep calling us

A custom house has a lot of people working on the same ceiling. We are used to
sitting down with the architect, the lighting designer, the builder and the
homeowner and settling what actually gets built before anyone frames a wall.
Getting that sorted early is most of what keeps a job on schedule.

- More than 30 years on residential custom homes
- We work directly with architects, designers, builders and homeowners
- Lighting is a specialty here, not a sideline
- Independently owned and operated since the start

# Notes and open questions

## Must resolve before launch

1. **License number.** California requires a contractor's licence number on
   advertising, including websites (B&P Code 7030.5). It is absent from the
   current site. BuildZoom lists 376352 for Heritage Electric and shows it as
   inactive when last checked. That is third party data and may be stale or the
   wrong entity. Verify at cslb.ca.gov before publishing anything.

2. **"Power to solar systems"** is ambiguous in the original. Written here as
   "power connections for solar systems." Confirm what it actually covers.

## Cut, and why

- **"Synergy - working together effectively on custom projects."** Says nothing
  the collaboration line does not already say.
- **"Be it your home or your workplace, or an open area, or any other small
  piece of storage area."** This contradicts the rest of the site. Everything
  else positions Heritage as a custom residential specialist working through
  builders. Confirm whether commercial work is still wanted.
- **"We are privileged to serve..."** and **"state of art"** language. Replaced
  with plain statements.
- **"Talking About Heritage Electric"** as a heading. Not a phrase anyone
  searches for or understands at a glance.

## Worth changing

- **"More than 30 years" is probably understated.** If Dave founded it in the
  early 1990s it is closer to 35 now. A founding year ("Wiring South Bay homes
  since 19XX") is stronger and does not go stale.
- **The partner.** "Founded by Dave Murray and partner" is vague. Name them or
  drop the reference.
- **Email.** dmurray@ is fine for a small site. An info@ alias ages better if
  anyone else ever answers mail.
- **Photographs.** For this kind of work, finished installations will carry the
  site further than any amount of copy.
